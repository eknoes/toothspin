ToothSpin

A Bluetooth speaker input for Music Assistant.

Alpha. This runs in one home on one dongle. Expect rough edges, breaking changes and setups that don't work yet. Bug reports are welcome.

Pair your phone with a USB Bluetooth dongle like you would with a speaker. Whatever the phone plays shows up in Music Assistant as a live input, and you can play it on any player or group from there.

Under the hood it's a Bluetooth A2DP sink that streams to Music Assistant as a Sendspin source. No Music Assistant fork or custom provider: it uses the sendspin_source provider that ships with Music Assistant 2.10.6.

What works

  • Several phones on one dongle, each its own input in Music Assistant.
  • SBC and AAC from the phone; PCM, FLAC or Opus to Music Assistant.
  • A small web UI for pairing, connecting, and picking which speakers a phone plays on.
  • Pairing only while you open a pairing window. No guest mode.

What you need

  • Music Assistant 2.10.6 or newer.
  • A Linux host with a Bluetooth dongle and bluetoothd, with no other Bluetooth audio stack on it.
  • Docker or Podman. The image brings its own PipeWire.
